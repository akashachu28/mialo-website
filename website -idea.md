Mialo.ai Homepage v1.0

Creative Direction & Manual Build Specification

Objective

The homepage should not look like an AI product website.

It should feel like the operating system for enterprise operations.

Every section should answer exactly one question. No section should repeat the previous one.

The experience should feel calm, premium, confident and timeless.

Overall Design Language

Visual Style

Dark premium theme
Apple × Linear × Palantir inspiration
Large typography
Large whitespace
Minimal UI
No dashboards
No fake analytics
No excessive gradients
No glowing everywhere
Motion should feel purposeful rather than decorative
Signature Design Language

Across the homepage there are only three signature animated elements.

1. Travelling Blue Pulse

This represents operational intelligence flowing through the system.

It appears in every animated section.

2. Intelligence Layer

The frosted glass intelligence component introduced in the Hero.

This exact component must be reused throughout the homepage.

Never redesign it.

3. Optical Ripple

Whenever the travelling pulse enters the Intelligence Layer:

subtle optical ripple
resembles light travelling through frosted glass
never resembles electricity
never resembles water
never flashy
This becomes the visual identity of Mialo.

Motion Guidelines

Only ONE primary animation per section.

Never animate everything.

Animations should be calm.

Never distracting.

Use the same:

speed
easing
pulse design
ripple
animation language
across every section.

Do NOT synchronise every section to pulse simultaneously.

Each section should animate independently while sharing identical motion language.

My final thought

We've spent quite a bit of time iterating, and I think the homepage is now built on a coherent narrative rather than a collection of attractive sections. That's a significant improvement. From here on, I'd resist the temptation to keep polishing individual components. Instead, focus on execution quality—spacing, typography, motion, and consistency. Those details will do more to elevate the site than another round of conceptual changes.
